The Four Novel Algorithmic Pillars of NCM-Net

👁️
PILLAR 1 · CROSS-MODAL INTEGRITY

Asymmetric Gated Cross-Modal Alignment (AG-CMA)

Standard multimodal systems naively concatenate text and image vectors, which causes severe accuracy degradation when citizens upload uninformative, blank, or mismatched photos. AG-CMA computes cross-modal semantic similarity and gates visual evidence through a dynamic sigmoid unit:

g = σ(6.0 · (Simcross(zt, zv) − 0.40)) · 𝕀(Simcross ≥ 0.35)
✔ Attenuates visual noise (g → 0 for blank/mismatched photos) & flags evidence fraud.
📊
PILLAR 2 · UNCERTAINTY QUANTIFICATION

Distribution-Free Mondrian Conformal Prediction

Standard machine learning classifiers output fragile point predictions with uncalibrated probabilities. NCM-Net implements split conformal risk control to generate mathematically certified prediction sets ℂ(X) with finite-sample statistical guarantees:

P(Y ∈ ℂ(X)) ≥ 1 − α  |  ℂ(X) = {y ∈ 𝒴 : P(y|X) ≥ 1 − q̂α}
✔ Singleton set |ℂ(X)|=1 enables autonomous routing; multi-label sets trigger supervisor triage.
⚡
PILLAR 3 · SAFETY REGULARIZATION

Neuro-Symbolic Safety Constraint Layer (FOL Axioms)

Pure neural models suffer from catastrophic false-negatives on rare hazardous emergencies due to majority-class frequency bias. We integrate a First-Order Logic (FOL) campus hazard ontology that projects continuous embeddings onto symbolic safety bounds:

∀x, ActiveSparkOrFire(x) ∧ PassengerMachinery(x) ⇒ Priority(x) ≥ High
✔ Guarantees 100% Zero-Miss Recall on life-safety, chemical, and elevator hazards.
🌐
PILLAR 4 · SPATIAL CASCADE MODELING

Spatio-Temporal Infrastructure Cascade Kernel (ST-ICK)

Standard 2D flat clustering treats tickets as independent. Campus utilities follow physical topological connectivity (power feeders, water mains, vertical risers). ST-ICK computes graph-diffusive spatio-temporal affinity between incidents:

Aij = Wtopo(vi, vj) · exp(−|ti − tj| / τ) · CosineSim(zi, zj)
✔ Clusters cascading complaints into a single root-cause diagnosis (e.g. 415V feeder trip).
